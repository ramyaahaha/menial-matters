on the concept of time slowing down near blackholes and the fundamental assumption that it is time passing through us, and time slowing down.

From the pov of us passing through time, how do you interpret it. Obviously if i pass by it passes by me, but the frame of interpretation changes. I am not really sure on how it would look from that frame, but i feel we need to look through it.

Because we don’t understand the randomness of our “feelings” and “consciousness” we don’t consider it physics-cally science enough.

Interpretations of time and how we experience time wrt to feelings and human cycles and natural cycles will definitely provide with a better intuition to understanding time than clock time ever will.  I do believe that nothing is random. Unless our definition of random is _our inability to determine the causality of events or reasoning for occurence of patterns_. 

Why does time only move forward? Well how do we know it only moves forward. I want our interpretations of time to be completely shattered and toppled. Like the revelation that earth revolves around the sun, and not the universe around the earth. 
I believe that is what will and only that will redo societies and governments and corporates. Capitalism thrives on monetising and exploiting from the concept of clock time. And that’s also why our efforts to predict climate change and consequences go off. Because clock time determines it all. 

If nothing at all even at a quantum level is bound to be uniform in terms of their fundamental behavior, why is time bound to behave so timelessly? 

Timeless means changeless. So if everything changes, why is our fundamental metric of determining change not subject to the change. Why is clock time not bound to change with time? Is it because we hold very dear to us the constant? 

And back to basics, the fact that time is affected by gravity, means by itself and is enough evidence at a greater scale and scheme of things that time isnt fundamental. Gravity is. The feeling of “towards something” is the fundamental. Natural way of things is to be still and eventually be driven apart because of our expanding universe, and opposite to the basic nature of the working of the universe is gravity, effort. 

This “towards” explains so much of how we define and yearn to experience love, passion, commitment, all of these fundamentally imply the effort “towards something”. Time isn’t the timeless. Gravity is. The feeling of “towards” is.

Why is it so though?
