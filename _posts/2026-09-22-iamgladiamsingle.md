---
title: I am glad I am single right now
---

10:38 am, Tuesday.

I feel like writing something and hence I will. 

I think I’ve been putting too much pressure on myself to get it all right and correct. I think I got used to the gratification, and the thrill of seeing the results. And the truth of the matter is that right now, I suck at it, all of it. And I say that not in a very self-depreciating tone or manner. Most of whatever I attempt, I am menially average or below average at it. Study? Problem solving? Aptitude? Vocabulary? GK? Painting? Dance? Running? Applications? Resume? Friends? Family? Sex?
Spanning across an arena of aspects, I am at most at the average of my potential. That part of making progress where you don’t see any progress. Very normal, very natural and maybe also familiar and yet so uncomfortable.

And I am really glad that I am single at the moment. I see why and how events have unfolded previously to get me here. I am glad that I am the only one who gets to witness me in this state. It’s an experience very selfishly purely mine to live through and experience. 

“Observational intimacy” they speak about. I understand now, maybe some of it, of how the rawness of witnessing a being with no guards up can do a being. It indeed is a very intimate experience. And definitely not the kind they show and speak of in movies. There’s intimacy in being around someone in their deepest, darkest most sowrrowful lows, without judgement. I wonder what witnessing myself here can do my own "me". It’s paradoxical in ways I can't entirely articulate yet. 

I also right now do believe that whatever I am writing is also at most average. I can experience gap of the lack of vocabulary between my thoughts and this piece of paper.

What a privilege it is to also responsible for my own self. I think I am growing to see how this part of adulting might actually be a gift, a very exhaustingly tiring gift to hold, but regardless a gift. There’s a certain ___ in being able to just be. As much as this gap from work and isolation often has started to seem like a curse, I am also helplessly aware of how this is also the best time of my life, with myself. 

And such a ___ it is to be exhausted by my own ambitions. The only person I am envious of right now is some parts the person I was and most parts the person I am yet to become. And that’s a one of a kind experience.

I did find myself cribbing about how one of the dudes I recently went out with dried it off for me by talking about his opinions on capitalism and right-wing politics. I had decided long back that I wouldn't do that anymore. Talk about societal concerns and global warming on first or second dates. They make the walks awkward and lame generally because of their incapabiity to hold or maneuver a conversation, leaving me fully aware of how I have to do yet again the work of keeping a conversation. 
And so maybe, the only one who can give me what I need right now, is me. I am not saying that I am the smartest around here, but what I am seeking for “someone to push me to be smarter than I am” is me.

And there’s a sense of security in knowing that at the end of it all, all I have to do is rely on myself. I can always talk to myself, hangout with myself, go out with myself, touch myself and just do as I please.

And that’s so cool.
