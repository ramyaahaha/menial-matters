Seduction and surrender 

Around about three weeks ago, today, I was being seduced by my own life, self and hence the universe. And, I’d want to believe, the since then I’ve only let it do so more. The intensity of seduction is directly proportional to the extent of surrender. Greater the extent of surrender to the source of that sensation, more intense the sensation. This true for all things. Dedication, I believe, towards anything is surrender in its purest form.  That’s what the monks say too. Give up trying to control the external world, and you’d be at peace. I hear, give in and surrender to life. Let it seduce your consciousness to experience being alive.

**Seduction and time** 

That braincell which knows there’s always going to be more and new information to take in. This is time agnostic. You revisit the past, there’s just going to be n+1 bits of information waiting for you to process it. You think of the future, there’s again fundamentally information waiting there for you to arrive at it and process it. And hence, PRESENT is infinite. Hence 
there’s always scope and space for (more) seduction. Nostalgia feels so good because it is being seduced by the past, and so does fantasy, its being seduced by future (or the idea of it). 
I believe, seduction encompasses love and passion than it being the other way around. Love doesn’t reason curiosity as much as seduction does. Present - Infinity – surrender – seduction 
all sound like the faces of the same dice. Seduction is the fundamental sensation keeping us going which manifests in the form of much more abstract and high-level human tendencies like greed, love and attraction, romance, passion, ambition etc. To sense is to feel and to feel is to experience -> experience is the highest faculty for our consciousness to navigate this blob of space and time. The base of it, is to sense – and be aware of it. Awareness is how one can practice surrendering control. And that’s what Vipassana tells you to do, and it makes sense why I feel seduced. All of this doesn’t seem like conformation bias to me. It makes sense to my bones now, why I like quantum stuff and science in general too! I am seduced by it. And for me, the scope of that isn’t limited either, and so naturally I seem to be drawn towards a LOT of things, with varying intensities. This makes so much sense. 

People who’re able to romanticize things and events; are in other words seduced by it. The concept of YOLO can now be viewed under this light, as an intellectual reminder for the conscious mind to surrender logic and patterns, and give in to the experience of being seduced for what is waiting for you to arrive at it.

_Seduction is how the fundamental "nothingness" of our universe manifests._

Seduction in the mathematics of emotions and sensations allows for more dimensions to exist within it, and hence I argue that it is more fundamental about the universe than love is. It’s such a natural connection, present oriented manifestation of human experience. What attachment is to the fundamental sensation of love, fantasy is to the fundamental sensation of seduction. The peak of them both is when you’re not hooked to wanting it more. The peak of it is when you’re using it as currency to live in the present, than to buy living in the past or the future. Present, point in time; they’re the rawest forms of experiencing existence and hence “life”. And now it makes sense why I’ve always felt drawn towards Charlie Puth and John Mayer music. There’s seduction in the art they create, the notes they use and the words they just partially hum. The graininess in John’s voice is his faculty of seducing others. Art is seduction. And so is expression. What’s it about live music that make it more life-like, and so full of experience than studio music. Seduction again. That one extra beat they skip, one extra high note they hit and whatever uncertainly different they do with their vocal chords while singing. That uncertainty makes it feel like a ride. Being on a ride. Riding. So much adventure just associated to that word. So much naughtiness and hence so much seduction.

_**Bell curve**_ – mathematical essence that most things in life are statistically average, while extremes are rare. Also known as a normal distribution, it is a graph shaped like a bell where 
the highest point represents the average, and the data tapers off symmetrically into "tails" on both ends. 

This is not a confirmation bias situation, because Bell curve’s here for my rescue. All of what I have attempted to explain and make sense of above, seems to me universally true, because I can fit it into a bell curve. It’s an integrable function. Parallels between probability density – observed values and extent of giving up control – time are pretty natural to me. And another point for, mathematics is the language of the universe.
